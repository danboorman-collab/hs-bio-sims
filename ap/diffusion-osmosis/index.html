AP Biology · Big Idea: Energetics · Topic 2.7

Diffusion and osmosis

What is the water potential of a piece of potato tissue? Seven stages, in the order a student works them — watch, design, collect, watch, analyze, conclude, answer.

What we change
The sucrose concentration: 0.0 to 1.0 M, six solutions
What we measure
The mass of each core before and after an hour, in grams
What we work out
The concentration at which a core neither gains nor loses

The analysis skill: Percent change in mass, a best-fit line, its zero crossing, and Ψs = −iCRT. The graph: a scatter plot with one best-fit straight line.
