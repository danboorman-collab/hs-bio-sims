AP Biology · Big Idea: Systems Interactions · Topic 8.7

Ecosystem disruption

Does the number of mayfly nymphs differ above and below a farm outflow? Seven stages, in the order a student works them — watch, design, collect, watch, analyze, conclude, answer.

What we compare
Two stretches of one stream, above and below the outflow
What we count
Mayfly nymphs in a three-minute kick sample
How many
Twelve samples at each site, at positions chosen at random

The analysis skill: Median, quartiles, a five-number summary, and what a box plot can and cannot say. The graph: two box-and-whisker plots on one axis.

Stage 5Simulation · Step by step, typed answers

Work the analysis

One step at a time, with your own answer typed into each box. Every miss gets a hint that says more than the last, and none of them gives the answer.

Stage 6Question · A guide, then one choice

Choose the conclusion

What a conclusion has to do — a claim, the evidence for it, and the reasoning that connects them — and then three conclusions for your own results.

Stage 7Questions · Eight items

Answer eight questions

On carrying the investigation out, on what its numbers come to, and on what they do and do not show.
