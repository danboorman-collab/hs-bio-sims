AP Biology · Big Idea: Energetics · Topic 3.1-3.2b

Enzyme catalysis

Does temperature change the rate of an enzyme-catalyzed reaction? Seven stages, in the order a student works them — watch, design, collect, watch, analyze, conclude, answer.

What we change
The temperature of the water bath: 20, 30, 40, 50 and 60 °C
What we measure
The volume of oxygen the gas syringe collects in one minute, in mL/min
How many
Five tubes at every temperature, so each mean can carry an error bar

The analysis skill: Mean, standard deviation, standard error, and the overlap of error bars. The graph: a line of means with ±2SE error bars.
