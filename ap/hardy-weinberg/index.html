AP Biology · Big Idea: Evolution · Topic 7.5

Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium

Is this population's gene pool behaving as the model says an undisturbed one would? Seven stages, in the order a student works them — watch, design, collect, watch, analyze, conclude, answer.

What we census
Five hundred snails, each placed in one of three genotype classes
What we calculate
p and q from the allele counts, then p², 2pq and q²
What we test
The gap between the census and what those proportions predict

The analysis skill: p and q from a census, the expected genotype counts, and chi-square against them. The graph: a census beside the Hardy–Weinberg expectation.
