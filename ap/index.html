Nine lab simulations

Two investigations for each of the four Big Ideas, and a different piece of AP data handling in every one. The 2025 CED prescribes no list of labs — it asks for 25% of instructional time on lab work and at least two investigations per Big Idea — so these were chosen to cover the four Big Ideas twice and to put the statistics the course teaches somewhere a student can use them.

What every one of them has

Seven stages, in the order a student works them: watch the method, design the investigation, collect the results, watch the analysis skill, work the analysis skill, choose the conclusion, answer eight questions.

Stage 2 is a set of decisions rather than an equipment tray, because AP's Science Practice 3 asks for the null hypothesis, the variables, the replicates and the control, and four of these nine have no bench to lay out. Stage 3 produces replicates, because a standard error needs more than one reading. Stage 5 asks for typed answers with a stated tolerance. Stage 6 follows Science Practice 6: a claim, the evidence for it, and the reasoning that connects them.

Where the numbers come from

Every data set was designed. The lesson outlines print worked numbers inside their statistics lessons — catalase at 25, 35 and 40 °C in mL/min, a potato crossing zero at 0.38 M, 88 against 112 flies — and a practical reusing those would be one a student could answer from memory. So each set here was designed to give one clean, defensible verdict, in the units, at the sample sizes and with the conventions the outlines use, and deliberately different from the numbers they print.

Six checks run on every build. verify.py recomputes each data set's design target from the readings. tolerance_check.py walks each chain the way a student carrying three significant figures would and confirms every box still accepts the answer. item_math_check.py reworks the arithmetic of every calculation item. number_check.py looks for every number in every student-facing string in the data behind it. conclusion_check.py and question_check.py screen the items for the tells that give a key away.

The videos

Two per practical, at about 85 seconds each. The enzyme pair was submitted first so that the house style can be checked against a finished render before the other sixteen are committed; a render takes between fifty minutes and four and a half hours.
