AP Biology · Big Idea: Information Storage and Transmission · Topic 4.6

Mitosis and the cell cycle

How long does a cell spend in each phase of its cycle? Seven stages, in the order a student works them — watch, design, collect, watch, analyze, conclude, answer.

What we count
Two hundred cells from an onion root tip, each scored for its phase
What we bring in
The length of the whole cycle, 24 hours, known from elsewhere
What we work out
The minutes each phase lasts

The analysis skill: Counts into shares, shares into times, and what the method assumes. The graph: a pie chart of the cycle.
