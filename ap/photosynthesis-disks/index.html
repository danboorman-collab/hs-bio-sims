AP Biology · Big Idea: Energetics · Topic 3.4

Photosynthesis

How does light intensity change the rate of photosynthesis? Seven stages, in the order a student works them — watch, design, collect, watch, analyze, conclude, answer.

What we change
The light intensity: five settings, and a dark control
What we measure
ET50, the time at which the fifth of ten leaf disks rises
What we work out
The rate as 1 / ET50, and where the curve levels off

The analysis skill: ET50, the reciprocal that turns a time into a rate, and a limiting factor. The graph: two quantities on one x axis, each with its own scale.

Design the investigation

The null hypothesis, what is measured, how many repeats, what is held constant and what the control is. One submission, then every option is marked and explained.

Stage 3Simulation · Fixed readings

Collect the results

Work through the conditions and read off what each one gives. Every student meets the same numbers, because a marked question needs one right answer.

Stage 4Video · about 85 seconds

Watch how to work the analysis

ET50, the reciprocal that turns a time into a rate, and a limiting factor.

Stage 5Simulation · Step by step, typed answers

Work the analysis

One step at a time, with your own answer typed into each box. Every miss gets a hint that says more than the last, and none of them gives the answer.

Stage 6Question · A guide, then one choice

Choose the conclusion

What a conclusion has to do — a claim, the evidence for it, and the reasoning that connects them — and then three conclusions for your own results.

Stage 7Questions · Eight items

Answer eight questions

On carrying the investigation out, on what its numbers come to, and on what they do and do not show.
