AP Biology · Big Idea: Systems Interactions · Topic 8.4

Population growth

How does a population grow when its resources are finite? Seven stages, in the order a student works them — watch, design, collect, watch, analyze, conclude, answer.

What we follow
A yeast population in a sealed flask, for sixteen hours
What we measure
Cell density in million cells per mL, every two hours
What we work out
r from the straight stretch, K from where the curve levels off

The analysis skill: The growth constant r from a log plot, the carrying capacity K, and the braking term. The graph: the same counts on a linear scale and on a log scale.
