Eight questions

Energetics · Topic 3.1-3.2b

On carrying this investigation out, and on what its results show. Each question is marked as soon as it is answered, and every option is explained.

Question 1 of 8

A class tests whether temperature changes the rate at which catalase releases oxygen from hydrogen peroxide. They set up five tubes at each of five temperatures and read the gas syringe after one minute.

Why does the class set up five tubes at each temperature rather than one?

Question 2 of 8

In the same investigation, one tube at each temperature holds hydrogen peroxide with no catalase added. It is read after one minute like all the others.

What does that tube establish?

Question 3 of 8

Five tubes of catalase at one temperature released oxygen at 2.6, 3.0, 2.8, 3.1 and 2.5 mL/min.

What is the mean rate, to two decimal places?

Question 4 of 8

A mean rate of 4.20 mL/min was worked out from six tubes. The standard deviation of those six readings was 0.294 mL/min, and the square root of six is 2.449.

Between which values does an error bar of ±2SE run?

Question 5 of 8

Two mean rates are drawn with error bars representing ±2SE. The first bar runs from 2.90 to 3.42 mL/min. The second runs from 3.31 to 3.95 mL/min.

What do these two bars show about the two conditions?

Question 6 of 8

A student measures catalase at 30 °C and at 40 °C, five tubes at each, and finds that the two ±2SE bars overlap. The student writes: ‘Catalase works at the same rate at 30 °C and 40 °C.’

What is wrong with that statement?

Question 7 of 8

A class repeats the catalase investigation with twenty tubes at each temperature instead of five. The spread of the readings comes out much the same as before.

What happens to the error bars?

Question 8 of 8

A different class measures how fast a protease from pineapple digests gelatin at two pH values, six tubes at each. At pH 5 the mean is 1.84 mg/min with a ±2SE bar from 1.70 to 1.98. At pH 7 the mean is 2.06 mg/min with a bar from 1.92 to 2.20.

What is the verdict on the null hypothesis that pH makes no difference to the rate?
