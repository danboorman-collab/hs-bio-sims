Eight questions

Information Storage and Transmission · Topic 5.3

On carrying this investigation out, and on what its results show. Each question is marked as soon as it is answered, and every option is explained.

Question 1 of 8

Two fruit flies heterozygous for body color and for wing shape are crossed. The two genes are on different chromosomes.

Which ratio of phenotypes does this cross predict among the offspring?

Question 2 of 8

A grower counts 480 offspring from a cross and sorts them into four phenotype classes before working out what the model predicts for each.

How many degrees of freedom does a chi-square test of these counts have?

Question 3 of 8

In one phenotype class a grower counted 112 seedlings where the model predicted 120.

What does this class contribute to chi-square?

Question 4 of 8

A dihybrid cross gave 640 offspring in four phenotype classes. Chi-square for the counts against a 9 : 3 : 3 : 1 model came to 1.51, and the critical value at three degrees of freedom and p = 0.05 is 7.81.

What is the verdict?

Question 5 of 8

A second cross, between two tomato plants heterozygous for stem color and fruit shape, gave 440 offspring: 300 purple stem with round fruit, 52 purple stem with long fruit, 48 green stem with round fruit and 40 green stem with long fruit. A 9 : 3 : 3 : 1 model predicts 247.5, 82.5, 82.5 and 27.5. Chi-square comes to 42.5.

What do these counts show, given a critical value of 7.81 at three degrees of freedom?

Question 6 of 8

A student finishes a chi-square test on a breeding cross, fails to reject the null hypothesis, and writes: ‘This proves the two genes assort independently.’

What is wrong with that?

Question 7 of 8

A breeder repeats the same cross and counts 160 offspring instead of 640. The offspring fall in almost exactly the same proportions as before.

What happens to chi-square?

Question 8 of 8

A poultry breeder crosses two birds and predicts that the chicks will be feathered and unfeathered in a 3 : 1 ratio. Of 200 chicks, 142 are feathered and 58 are not. Chi-square for these counts is 1.71.

Using a critical value of 3.84 at one degree of freedom, what is the verdict?
