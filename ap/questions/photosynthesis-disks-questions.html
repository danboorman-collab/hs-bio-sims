Eight questions

Energetics · Topic 3.4

On carrying this investigation out, and on what its results show. Each question is marked as soon as it is answered, and every option is explained.

Question 1 of 8

Leaf disks are cut with a hole punch, put in a syringe of sodium hydrogen carbonate solution, and the plunger drawn back with the nozzle sealed.

Why are the disks put under vacuum before the investigation begins?

Question 2 of 8

Ten leaf disks are put in each beaker, and the time is taken when the fifth has risen to the surface.

Why is the time to half the disks used rather than the time to the first one?

Question 3 of 8

At one light intensity, half of ten leaf disks had risen after 12.5 minutes.

What is the rate, expressed as 1 / ET50?

Question 4 of 8

A class measures ET50 at five light intensities and finds that the rate rises steeply at first and then hardly changes between the two brightest settings.

What does the flat part of the curve show?

Question 5 of 8

A flask of water is placed between the lamp and the beaker of leaf disks.

What is it there for?

Question 6 of 8

A student runs the investigation with disks cut from a thick, waxy leaf and finds that no disk rises within forty minutes at any light intensity.

What is the most likely explanation?

Question 7 of 8

Two sets of leaf disks are run at the same light intensity. One beaker holds sodium hydrogen carbonate solution; the other holds distilled water with a drop of detergent, as the first does.

What is the distilled water beaker for?

Question 8 of 8

A student investigates how the colour of light changes the rate, running leaf disks under red, green and blue filters at the same brightness. ET50 comes out at 7.2 minutes under red, 20.0 under green and 6.8 under blue.

What do these times show?
