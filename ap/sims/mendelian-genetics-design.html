Information Storage and Transmission · Topic 5.3

The cross has been set up and the offspring are about to be sorted. Five decisions first. Choose one answer in each, then submit once.

Decision 1 of 5

Which of these is the null hypothesis for this cross?

Decision 2 of 5

Which parents give a 9 : 3 : 3 : 1 ratio among the offspring?

Decision 3 of 5

Why are more than 600 offspring counted rather than 60?

Decision 4 of 5

How are the offspring sorted?

Decision 5 of 5

Why is each fly sorted before its class is tallied against the expected numbers?

Choose one answer in each of the 5 decisions.
