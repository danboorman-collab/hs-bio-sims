Design the investigation

Evolution · Topic 7.9

A gene has been sequenced in five species. Five decisions first. Choose one answer in each, then submit once.

Decision 1 of 5

Why is the same gene compared in all five species rather than five different genes?

Decision 2 of 5

What is compared between each pair of species?

Decision 3 of 5

Two species share more of this sequence than two others do. What does that suggest?

Decision 4 of 5

Why is one species known to be distantly related included at all?

Decision 5 of 5

What can this comparison not settle?

Choose one answer in each of the 5 decisions.
