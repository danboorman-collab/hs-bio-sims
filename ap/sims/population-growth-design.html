Systems Interactions · Topic 8.4

A flask of yeast is about to be sampled every two hours. Five decisions first. Choose one answer in each, then submit once.

Decision 1 of 5

Which of these is the testable question?

Decision 2 of 5

How is the number of cells found at each time?

Decision 3 of 5

Why are samples taken every two hours rather than once at the start and once at the end?

Decision 4 of 5

What is held the same through the 16 hours?

Decision 5 of 5

Why will the counts also be plotted as their natural logarithms?

Choose one answer in each of the 5 decisions.
