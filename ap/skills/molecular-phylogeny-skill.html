Step 1 — from a percentage to a count

A percent identity of 90% over 500 positions means the two sequences differ at 10% of them, which is 50 positions.

Why this step: A percentage is easy to compare but hard to picture. Turning it into a number of positions says what actually differs between the two proteins, and it is the differences, not the percentages, that build up at a steady rate over time.
