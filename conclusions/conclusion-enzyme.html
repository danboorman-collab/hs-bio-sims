Write your conclusion

A conclusion says what the results show. It is not a summary of what you did.

A worked example, from a different investigation

Seedlings were grown at five temperatures and their height measured after two weeks.

As the temperature rose from 10 °C to 25 °C the seedlings grew taller, from 4 cm at 10 °C to 11 cm at 25 °C. Above 25 °C they grew less well, reaching only 6 cm at 30 °C. So these seedlings grow tallest at about 25 °C. The results do not show what happens above 30 °C, because no higher temperature was tested.

Now write your own

Does temperature change the rate of an enzyme reaction?

0 words

The model answer

As the temperature rose from 10 °C to 40 °C the reaction went faster, from a mean of 5 bubbles in one minute at 10 °C to 25 at 40 °C. Above 40 °C the rate fell sharply, to 7 bubbles at 50 °C. So this enzyme works fastest at about 40 °C. The results do not show what happens between 40 and 50 °C, because no temperature between them was tested.
