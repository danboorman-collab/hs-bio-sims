The method. An enzyme breaks hydrogen peroxide down, and the oxygen given off comes off as bubbles. The rate is the number of bubbles counted in one minute.

The reaction runs in a test tube standing in a water bath, warmed to 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50 °C in turn. The thermometer's bulb sits in the water, so it reads the bath rather than the mixture.

The same volume of hydrogen peroxide and the same volume of enzyme solution are used every time.
