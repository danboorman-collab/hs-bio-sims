What every one of them has

Seven stages, in the order a student works them: watch the method, set up or plan, collect the results, watch the analysis skill, work the analysis skill, choose the conclusion, answer eight questions.

Stage 2 is an equipment tray in the bench practicals and a set of survey decisions in the two field ones, because a field survey has four or five objects and a great many decisions, and both survey videos spend their whole length on the decisions. No two practicals teach the same analysis skill — the column above is the check on that.

What is assessed

TimeBack cannot see inside a simulation, so the simulations are the practical and what a student is assessed on is the eight questions at the end, together with the checkpoints in the lessons each practical belongs to. Every reading is the same for every student, because a marked question needs one right answer.
