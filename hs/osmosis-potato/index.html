Required practical · High School Biology

Osmosis in potato

Does the concentration of the solution around a potato cylinder change its mass? Seven stages, in the order a student works through them — watch, set up, measure, plot, conclude, answer.

What we change
The concentration of the sucrose solution: 0, 5, 10, 15 and 20%
What we measure
The mass of each cylinder, before and after
What we keep the same
The potato, the size of cylinder, the volume of solution, and the 30 minutes

Watch the method

Set out the equipment

Choose what the method needs. One submission, then every piece is marked and explained.

Stage 3Simulation · Five concentrations, weighed twice

Collect the results

Weigh the cylinder, leave it thirty minutes, weigh it again. One collection at each concentration, and then it locks.

Stage 4Video · 86 seconds

Watch how to draw the graph

Where the axes go when results fall below zero, and why this graph takes a straight line rather than a curve.

Stage 5Simulation · Axes, five points, one line

Plot the results

Then read off the concentration where the line crosses zero — the answer the practical exists to find.

Stage 6Question · A worked example, then one choice

Choose the conclusion

What do the results show? A conclusion from another investigation, with what makes it good, and then three conclusions for your own results.

Stage 7Questions · Eight items

Answer eight questions

On carrying the practical out, and on what its results show.

Nothing here is marked behind the scenes. The simulations are the practical; what a student is assessed on is the eight questions at the end.

This practical's data was designed, not taken from a lesson. Unit 2 teaches osmosis across four lessons but none of them sets out an experiment or prints results, so these numbers were written for the practical and are now the source for the collecting stage, the plotting stage, the graph video and two of the questions.
