Required practical · High School Biology

Photosynthesis and light

Does brighter light make a plant photosynthesize faster? Seven stages, in the order a student works through them — watch, set up, measure, spot, conclude, answer.

What we change
The brightness of the lamp: six settings, 1 to 6
What we measure
Bubbles of oxygen counted in one minute
What we keep the same
The piece of pondweed, the distance from the lamp, the volume of water, and the minute

What do the results show? The three steps a conclusion takes, your own results back on the page, and three conclusions to choose between.

Stage 7Questions · Eight items

Answer eight questions

On carrying the practical out, and on what its results show.

Nothing here is marked behind the scenes. TimeBack cannot see inside a simulation, so the simulations are the practical and what a student is assessed on is the eight questions at the end, together with the checkpoints in the lessons this practical belongs to.

The readings are the same for every student, because a marked question needs one right answer. The analysis skill this practical teaches is spotting an anomalous result, and no two of the six practicals teach the same one.
