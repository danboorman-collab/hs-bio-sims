Required practical · High School Biology

Estimating a population

How many plants of one species are growing in a field too large to count? Seven stages, in the order a student works through them — watch, plan, count, scale up, conclude, answer.

What we count
Plants of one species inside a quadrat of 0.25 m²
How many
Ten quadrats, at ten pairs of random numbers
What we work out
A mean per quadrat, scaled up to a field of 800 m²

Nothing here is marked behind the scenes. TimeBack cannot see inside a simulation, so the simulations are the practical and what a student is assessed on is the eight questions at the end, together with the checkpoints in the lessons this practical belongs to.

The readings are the same for every student, because a marked question needs one right answer. The analysis skill this practical teaches is scaling a sample up to an estimate, and no two of the six practicals teach the same one.
