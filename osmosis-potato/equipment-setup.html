The method. Cylinders of potato are cut from one potato with a cork borer and trimmed to the same length. Each is blotted dry and weighed, then left in a sucrose solution for 30 minutes, blotted dry the same way, and weighed again.

Five solutions are used: 0, 5, 10, 15 and 20% sucrose, the same volume of each, one cylinder in each tube, all five left together for the same 30 minutes at room temperature.

What is worked out from the two weighings is the percentage change in the mass of each cylinder.
