The method. Pondweed in a test tube of water gives off oxygen when it photosynthesizes, and the oxygen comes off the cut end of the stem as bubbles. The rate is the number of bubbles counted in one minute.

The lamp has six brightness settings, and the brightness is the one thing that changes. The tube stays the same distance from the lamp every time.

A bright lamp gives off heat as well as light, and warming the water would change the rate on its own. So a beaker of water stands between the lamp and the tube: it takes the heat out and lets the light through.
